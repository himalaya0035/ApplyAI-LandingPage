Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: March 09, 2026


At ApplyAI, we strive to ensure our customers are fully satisfied with our automated career platform. Please review our policy regarding cancellations and refunds carefully.


1. Subscription Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription at any time directly through your account dashboard or by contacting our support team. Upon cancellation, your subscription will remain active until the end of the current paid billing cycle. No further recurring charges will be made to your payment method once canceled.


2. Refund Eligibility & Digital Goods Policy

Due to the immediate nature of digital automation services and server costs incurred upon use, we enforce a strict No Refunds policy for all subscription charges that have already been processed. We do not offer prorated refunds for canceled subscriptions.

Exceptions: We may, at our sole discretion, grant a refund in the following extraordinary circumstances:

  • A critical system failure on our end prevented the core functionality of the Service from working for an extended, continuous period (exceeding 48 hours).
  • You were charged erroneously due to a billing system glitch on our end.

3. Requesting a Refund

If you believe you qualify for an exceptional refund, you must initiate the request within 7 days of the disputed transaction date by emailing support@applyai.com. Please include your registered email address, transaction ID, and a detailed explanation of the issue.


4. Refund Processing Times

If your refund request is approved, the refund will be processed and credited automatically back to your original method of payment (e.g., Credit Card, UPI, Net Banking) via our payment gateway (e.g., Razorpay). Funds typically reflect in your account within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your financial institution.


5. Chargebacks and Disputes

We request that you contact us directly to resolve any billing issues before initiating a chargeback with your bank. Unwarranted chargebacks may result in the immediate and permanent suspension of your ApplyAI account.
