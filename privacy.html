Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 09, 2026


This Privacy Policy describes how Apply Pilot ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your Personal Information when you visit or use our services at applypilot.com (the "Site") and our related software applications.


1. Information We Collect

Personal Information: We collect information that you explicitly provide to us, including your name, email address, phone number, physical address, and uploaded documents (such as your resume and profile data).

Payment Information: All payment processing is handled by secure, PCI-compliant third-party payment gateways (e.g., Razorpay). We do not store full credit card numbers or backend financial credentials on our servers.

Usage Data: We automatically collect certain information about your device, including information about your web browser, IP address, time zone, and some of the cookies that are installed on your device.


2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to provide, maintain, and improve our career automation services. This includes securely storing your resume, matching you to jobs, filling out applications on your behalf, providing customer support, and processing your subscription billing securely.


3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share your data with necessary trusted third-party service providers (such as payment gateways, hosting providers, and job application platforms) solely for the purpose of executing our core services. These third parties are bound by strict data protection agreements.


4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track the activity on our Service and hold certain information. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent.


5. Data Security

We implement a variety of industry-standard security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.


6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to access, alter, restrict processing, and request deletion of your data at any time. To exercise these rights or withdraw your consent, please contact our Data Protection Officer via our support channels.
